# TunCon_Info

Gives unconscious players useful information instead of just a black screen.
Everything can be configured through CBA Settings.

- Shows your current condition, including bleeding, pulse, stable vitals, triage and cardiac arrest.
- Shows wake-up chance and epinephrine boost, so you actually know if waking up is realistic.
- Shows the closest friendly and closest medic nearby.
- Can also show distance to nearby help.
- Shows if you are inside a vehicle.
- Shows if the closest friendly or medic is inside a vehicle.
- Shows when someone is treating you while unconscious.
- Shows a notification when someone treats you while you are awake.
- Moan while unconscious.
- In CBA Settings, you can toggle each part separately, choose detailed or simple treatment feedback, and adjust search distance, update interval, text delays and the no-friendlies warning text.
- English fully supported. Spanish and Japanese translations are currently partial.

## Requirements

- ACE3
- CBA_A3
